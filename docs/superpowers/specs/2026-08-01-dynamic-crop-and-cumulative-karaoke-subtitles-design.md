# Dynamic Speaker Tracking Crop & Cumulative Karaoke Subtitles Design

## 1. Overview
This design addresses two key rendering issues in video clip generation:
1. **Dynamic Speaker Tracking Crop**: Replacing static single-coordinate cropping with multi-point temporal face tracking and smoothed panning interpolation in FFmpeg.
2. **Cumulative Word-by-Word Subtitles**: Replacing flickering single-word events with cumulative word reveals (current active word highlighted in yellow, preceding words in white within the active phrase) and seamless timing bridges.

---

## 2. Component 1: Cumulative Karaoke Subtitles

### 2.1 Problem Analysis
- Previously, subtitles were generated by emitting one ASS `Dialogue` event per word covering only `[word.start, word.end]`.
- Natural pauses or gaps between speech led to empty periods where all subtitles disappeared, causing rapid blinking.
- Extremely short word durations caused the yellow highlight to flash imperceptibly or skip.

### 2.2 Solution Architecture
- **Phrase-based Chunking**: Words are grouped into short readable chunks (3 to 6 words or punctuation boundaries `. , ! ?`).
- **Cumulative Word Build-Up**:
  - Each chunk starts at `chunk[0].start` and ends at `chunk[-1].end + 0.3s` (hold duration).
  - For each word $i$ in the chunk:
    - Text rendered: `Word_0 ... Word_{i-1} {\c&H00FFFF&}Word_i{\c}`.
    - Start timestamp: `word[i].start`.
    - End timestamp: `min(word[i+1].start, word[i].end + 0.2s)` (bridges small gaps without flickering).
    - Minimum duration clamp: at least 180ms highlight display duration.
- **ASS Styling**:
  - Bold sans-serif font (Arial/default).
  - Primary color: White (`&H00FFFFFF&`).
  - Active word highlight color: Bright Yellow (`&H00FFFF&` in BGR ASS format).
  - Outline: Heavy black stroke (`Outline=3`, `Shadow=2`) to ensure high contrast against any video background.

## 3. Component 2: Dynamic Speaker Tracking & Smooth Panning

### 3.1 Problem Analysis
- Previously, `detect_primary_face_center` sampled 10 frames and computed a single static median X coordinate.
- When the speaker moved across the frame or gesture-shifted, the camera remained fixed, causing the speaker to drift off-frame or get cut off.

### 3.2 Solution Architecture
- **Temporal Face Sampling**:
  - Sample video frames every 0.5 – 1.0s across the clip window `[start_time, end_time]`.
  - For each sample timestamp $t_k$, detect face center coordinate $X(t_k)$. If no face is detected in a sample, carry over the last known coordinate or default to frame center (0.5).
- **Trajectory Smoothing (EMA / Moving Average)**:
  - Apply an exponential moving average or sliding window filter over $X(t)$ to eliminate sudden jumps/jitter while maintaining natural panning responsiveness.
  - Clamp coordinates to safe boundaries so the crop box never exceeds $[0, \text{frame\_w} - \text{crop\_w}]$.
- **FFmpeg Expression Generation**:
  - Convert smoothed keypoints $(t_k, x_k)$ into an FFmpeg dynamic evaluation expression for the `crop` filter parameter `x`.
  - Using piecewise linear interpolation between keypoints:
    $$\text{crop}=\text{out\_w}:\text{out\_h}:x='f(t)':y=0$$
  - This enables native per-frame smooth camera panning without breaking the clip into multiple encoded segments.

## 4. Verification & Testing Strategy
- **Unit Tests**:
  - Test `words_to_karaoke_ass` with sample Whisper JSON data containing varied speech pauses and short timestamps to verify ASS dialogue continuity.
  - Test temporal sampling and smoothing functions in `crop_utils.py` to ensure boundary compliance and jitter reduction.
- **Integration/Render Test**:
  - Render a test clip in `9:16`, `1:1`, and `4:5` aspect ratios.
  - Verify smooth panning motion and consistent yellow-to-white word-by-word subtitle display without screen flickering.
